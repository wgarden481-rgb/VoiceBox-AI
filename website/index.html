How it works

Simple, reliable background assistant. Nothing to configure on the website — everything important is shown inside the app after you download.

⬇

1 — Download & run once

Download VoiceBox-Setup.exe (like Claude) and double-click. Instantly it shows “Downloading…”, then asks Microphone?, Screen?, Background? — click Allow/Yes, it installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\VoiceBox and opens the 8-step tour.

◎

2 — Follow the step-by-step tour

The app opens a guided tour: language, voice, hotkeys, permissions, AI, Gmail — one step at a time with Next/Back.

◐

3 — It lives in the tray

After the tour it tucks into the system tray, idle at 0.1% CPU, ready instantly.

▣

4 — Press and ask

Ctrl+Alt to ask anything, Ctrl+Shift to capture + ask about your screen. Card appears, speaks, then hides.

What it can do

All of this is explained in detail inside the app’s step-by-step tour. No examples to try here — try them with your voice and screen after download.

🎙️ Ask anything by voice

Hold Left Ctrl + Left Alt, speak naturally, get a spoken answer + text in a calm top card. History is saved.

🖥️ Analyze what you see

Hold Left Ctrl + Left Shift → screen captured → ask “what does this say?” or “summarize this” → answers about your screen.

🗣️ Nicer, cleaner voice

Neural voices (Aria, Jenny, Guy…) via Edge TTS, clean prosody, language-matched. You pick and test in the tour.

🌐 9 languages

English (US/UK), Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese — ask in one, answers in same.

🤖 Free AI that writes

Free Llama 3.1 via Groq (free key) or Ollama offline, plus Wikipedia/web fallback. It can write essays, paragraphs, stories when you ask.

📧 Gmail (optional, secure)

Say “check my email” — connects via Google OAuth 2.0, token stays on your PC, revoke anytime. Set up in the tour.

FAQ

Will it ask to make changes to my computer?

Yes, once. The installer triggers Windows UAC (“Do you want to allow this app to make changes?”). Click Yes — needed once to register global hotkeys and enable background start. You can also right-click → Run as administrator.

Is the AI free forever?

Yes. Free Llama 3.1 via Groq (free key in 30 sec at console.groq.com) or Ollama offline (ollama run llama3.1, no internet, unlimited). Even with no key, Wikipedia/DuckDuckGo + offline writer handle facts and can write essays/stories like AI.

How does screen analyze work?

Left Ctrl + Left Shift captures via mss, shows a thumbnail in the top card, then analyzes with Groq Vision / Ollama LLaVA if available, falling back to OCR text + Llama (free, offline). Ask “what does this say?” or “summarize”.

Which languages and voices?

9 languages listed above. Voices: neural Edge TTS (Aria, Jenny, Guy, Sonia…) + system SAPI5. You choose and test in the in-app tour.
